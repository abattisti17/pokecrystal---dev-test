# Exhaustive Beta Pokémon Catalog — All Generations
## New Section 2A: Pre-Spaceworld '97 "Tier 0" Designs

### What This Is

Before the famous Spaceworld '97 demo, Game Freak had designed 151 entirely new Pokémon for Gold & Silver. There wasn't enough space for all of them, so 51 were cut before the Spaceworld demo went public — Tajiri confirmed as much in a 1997 interview. These 51 cut designs survive only as black-and-white sprites with no names, types, or stats — the earliest lost Pokémon in history.

These designs were uncovered in April 2020 and only have black-and-white sprites, with no information known beyond their art. They were likely scrapped incredibly early into development and may have never been fully implemented. They are the earliest scrapped Pokémon of the second generation, dating to early 1997.

### GEN 1 — Pokémon Red & Green (1996)
**Source:** Helix Chamber leak (Feb 2019) — back sprites only from an early Red/Green build, plus the Satoshi Tajiri biographical manga (2018)

Originally, Pokémon Red and Green were supposed to have 190 Pokémon. However, 39 were cut due to cartridge limitations.

The MissingNo. slots in the internal index correspond directly to these cut Pokémon — MissingNo. was never Ho-Oh, Lugia, Nidogod, or Pikablu. It was the deleted Pokémon.

### GEN 2 — Pokémon Gold & Silver (1999)

Already cataloged exhaustively in the previous design bible. Key summary:

- **SW97 Demo (41+ named & typed):** Honooguma/Borubeaa/Dainabea, Kurusu/Akua/Akueria, Happa/Hanamogura, Kotora/Raitora, Rinrin/Berurun, Norowara/Kyonpan, Urufuman/Waaurufu, Mikon, Gyopin, Hinaazu, Jaranra, Shibirefugu, Madaamu, Tsuinzu, Purakkusu, Bomushikaa, Nameeru, Baririina, Animon, Taaban, Manboo/Ikari/Gurotesu, Monja, Para, Betobebii, Puchikoon, Kōnya, Bohboh/Booboo, Riifi, Gongu, and more.
- The final game also had data for four Pokémon without sprites — evolutions for Yanma, Girafarig, Shuckle, and Piloswine. Of these, Yanma and Piloswine later received evolutions in Gen 4.

**SW99 Demo Additions** (closer to final, fewer cuts):
- Dunsparce had two different designs; Wooper looked fairly different; Wobbuffett developed from what appears to be the Girafarig twin-ghost pre-evolution (Tsuinzu)
- A Pokémon based on a koala and a flower-faced Pokémon were present; fans have since noted we got Komala and Cherrim in later gens
- Kingdra, Sneasel, Tyrogue, and Hitmontop were mostly unchanged from SW97

### GEN 4 — Pokémon Diamond & Pearl (2006)
**Source:** 2019 beta sprite leak + 2020 Gigaleak

The Gen 4 leak wasn't on the same scale as Spaceworld '97 — none of the hundreds of beta sprites have significantly different designs compared to release. But there is one massive thing: lots and lots of scrapped gender differences.

It appears Game Freak was originally planning to give almost every Pokémon unique attributes based on gender. Many differences are subtle — female Bulbasaur have larger spots, female Cyndaquil family members have darker snouts. Most starter Pokémon exhibit small differences.

The reason they were likely scrapped: Gen 4 sprites were animated. Animating two full sets of sprites for every Pokémon would have been a massive undertaking.

**Notable Gen 4 gender differences that were cut:**
- Female Bulbasaur: larger spots
- Female Cyndaquil line: darker snouts
- Most starters had visible sex differentiation
- Dozens of Gen 1–3 Pokémon had planned female variants that were quietly dropped

**Recycled from Gen 2 beta:** Several Gen 4 Pokémon were concepts first seen in the Gold & Silver Spaceworld beta — Game Freak doesn't just discard unused ideas but holds onto them until a later date, including Leafeon (from Riifi), Lickilicky (from Nameeru), Mr. Mime Jr. (from Baririina), Yanmega (from unnamed data slot), and Mamoswine (from unnamed data slot).

### GEN 5 — Pokémon Black & White (2010)
**Source:** Ken Sugimori Nintendo Dream magazine interviews (translated by Dr. Lava) + 2024 Teraleak

Currently only seven Pokémon from Gen 5 have been revealed by the Teraleak as cut content, likely because Gen 5 had a fully formed design philosophy from the start seeking to remix iconic Kanto designs.

| Name | Description | Fate |
|---|---|---|
| **Snail Line** (Tanisu/Taniino/Taniking) | A family of snails originally designed as Karrablast's rival species — Karrablast the beetle would eat the snail Pokémon | Scrapped when Shelmet was brought in to fill the role; the beetle-steals-shell concept replaced the beetle-eats-snail concept |
| **Kaburion** | A defunct evolution of Karrablast with a dinosaur-like appearance | Cut when final Escavalier design was settled |
| **Garock** | A dinosaur-like Pokémon with Rock typing, resembling Carracosta | Likely scrapped Fossil Pokémon concept |
| **Gurokken** | Scrapped early in the design process; bears little resemblance to existing Pokémon | — |
| **Awanezumi** | Scrapped early; bears little resemblance to existing Pokémon | — |
| **Petilil Middle Stage** | The Petilil family was originally three stages; one was scrapped due to type balance (Gen 5 had too many Grass types) | No artwork released |
| **Tank Dragons** (proto-Hydreigon) | The Hydreigon line initially began as the Tank Dragons — likely a Steel/Dragon combination | Redesigned into the final dragon line |
| **Proto-Meloetta** | Meloetta started as a cute bug creature and lost all its bug elements during development | — |
| **Throh** (original) | Throh was scrapped due to its appearance being too similar to Tornadus, which had a similar color scheme. Changes were made to Tornadus and Thundurus to distinguish them from Throh and Sawk | Redesigned |
| **Crustle** (original) | Crustle's original concept was half Fire-type | Became pure Rock type |

### GEN 6 — Pokémon X & Y (2013)
**Source:** 2024 Teraleak

The Teraleak included dozens of concept art pieces showing early designs for beloved Gen 6 creatures and scrapped Pokémon that never made it across the finish line.

Known cuts/redesigns:
- **Beta Florges line:** An earlier design was found; community noted a skull cassowary concept was scrapped from this line
- **Beta Noibat:** Multiple color scheme iterations were tried before the final design
- **Beta Flabébé/Floette/Florges:** Concept art with early iterations found
- **Cut Mega Jynx:** A cut Mega Evolution for the original Jynx was uncovered — no model has been found yet
- **Beta peanut dragon:** A small dragon design with a rounded peanut-shaped body that was cut — community considers it a highlight loss
- One of Pokémon Legends: Z-A's most important items may have first surfaced 12 years ago as unreleased content in X and Y, suggesting a certain Mega Evolution was in the works for over a decade

### GEN 7 — Pokémon Sun & Moon (2016)
**Source:** Datamining + Teraleak

- From Generation III through Generation VII, hidden event-exclusive Mythical Pokémon were discovered almost immediately after release via datamining — including Hoopa (found in X/Y) and Marshadow (found in Sun/Moon)
- Various beta forms of Alolan Pokémon existed with different type combinations before the final Alolan forms were settled
- No major roster-level cuts comparable to earlier gens have been confirmed yet

### GEN 8 — Pokémon Sword & Shield (2019) / Legends: Arceus (2022)
**Source:** 2025 Teraleak 2

In October 2025, a second batch of Teraleak information was revealed, dubbed "Teraleak 2." This included cut content from Sword and Shield, Scarlet and Violet, and Legends: Z-A, as well as information about the future of the series going all the way into 2030.

- Cut content from Sword & Shield is still being actively researched as of early 2026
- The Galar Pokédex controversy ("Dexit") suggests many more Pokémon were planned and cut than have been confirmed
