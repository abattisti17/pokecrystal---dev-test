**POKÉMON: LOST LEGENDS**

Game Design Bible • v0.1

*The stories they never told. The Pokémon they hid. The myths made
real.*

**1. Vision Statement**

Pokémon: Lost Legends is a Gen 1/2 style ROM hack built on the
pokecrystal decompilation. It fills in the canonical gaps left by the
main series --- the anime storylines that never made it into a game, the
beta Pokémon that were cut before release, and the playground myths and
rumors that a generation of kids swore were real.

The guiding philosophy is: what if Pokémon took its own lore seriously?
The dark tones of the manga, the emotional weight of the anime movies,
and the mysterious corners of Gen 1/2 that were always implied but never
explored --- all brought to life in an authentic GBC experience.

Think of it as the \'Mario: The Lost Levels\' of Pokémon. Harder,
stranger, more complete. Made for people who grew up with these games
and have always wanted the version that existed in their imagination.

**2. Four Core Pillars**

**Pillar 1: Anime & Manga Stories**

These are the canonical events that shaped the Pokémon world but were
never playable:

-   The Porygon Incident --- the banned episode. An in-game event
    recreates it: a mysterious broadcast in Goldenrod City\'s Radio
    Tower causes widespread Pokémon illness. You must investigate.

-   Sabrina\'s Origin (manga) --- She wasn\'t always cold. Her backstory
    involves a traumatic psychic awakening that split her personality.
    Unlockable via a hidden quest in Saffron.

-   The War of Twenty Years Ago --- Lt. Surge, Koga, and Agatha all
    served. Environmental storytelling and optional veteran NPCs reveal
    what really happened.

-   Mewtwo\'s Creation --- The Cinnabar Lab notes exist in Gen 1, but
    here you find the researchers\' full journal and can witness the
    experiment in a flashback sequence.

-   Bill\'s Self-Experiment --- You find a second \'human-Pokémon\'
    capsule in Bill\'s Sea Cottage. His full diary is readable. A hidden
    form of Bill exists as a secret encounter.

-   The Marowak of Pokémon Tower --- In the original, her ghost is
    vague. Here, you learn her full name, her child\'s name (Cubone\'s),
    and can find Cubone\'s hidden item from his mother.

-   The Haunted Channels (anime ep. \'Ghost of Maiden\'s Peak\') ---
    Lavender Town has an expanded ghost subplot with a real resolution.

-   Ash\'s Journey Retold --- The player\'s rival has Ash\'s original
    team (Pikachu, Bulbasaur, Charizard, Snorlax, Lapras, Kingler).
    He\'s harder, cocky at first, then humble. His arc mirrors the
    anime.

**Pillar 2: Beta Pokémon**

The Gold/Silver beta leak (the 1997 Space World demo) contains over 40
scrapped Pokémon. Many were clearly full designs with names, types, and
evolutions. They deserve to exist.

-   Beta starters --- The original GS starters were a fire lion, a water
    croc, and a grass chimp (precursors to Entei, Feraligatr, and
    Chikorita\'s family). Alternate starter choice unlocks these.

-   Bubbles (proto-Marill) --- Originally a scrapped electric/water
    type. Found via a specific surfing route at night.

-   Happa --- A small grass Pokémon that was cut. Becomes the \'secret\'
    early-route find.

-   The scrapped Eeveelution --- A beta Normal-type Eevee evolution
    existed. Obtainable via a special item found in Ecruteak City.

-   Kurusu (scrapped dark cat) --- Precursor to Sneasel\'s line. Found
    in Ice Path.

-   Proto-Togetic --- The original Togetic design was a small egg-bird.
    Available as an alternate Togepi evolution path via old friendship
    mechanic.

-   All beta Pokémon have lore: NPCs, journal entries, or Pokédex
    entries explain why they were \'lost\' --- canonizing them as rare,
    hidden species.

**Pillar 3: Myths Made Real**

Every playground rumor gets a real in-game implementation. The game
teaches you these \'secrets\' through NPCs who speak in the breathless
way kids used to:

-   Mew Under the Truck --- The S.S. Aqua has a truck. If you have Cut,
    Surf, and a specific item (Old Lure), you can reach the underside.
    Mew is level 5. It knows only Pound and Barrier. The encounter plays
    a unique jingle.

-   Pressing B to Catch --- An old fisherman NPC tells you pressing B at
    the right moment \'steadies your hand.\' It\'s actually implemented:
    pressing B during the catch animation gives +5% catch rate. The game
    never officially confirms this.

-   Pikablu --- Marill is called \'Pikablu\' by a kid in Goldenrod. He
    tells you it lives \'past the waterfall where nobody goes.\' Finding
    it requires Waterfall HM and a specific time of day.

-   The Pokégods --- An NPC creepypasta collector in Lavender Town has a
    full journal of \'Pokégods.\' Each one is implemented as a
    ultra-rare encounter (Yoshi, Rainer, Flareth) with unique sprites.

-   The Haunted Lavender Town Music --- Entering Pokémon Tower at night
    with the volume up causes a rare visual glitch effect. An NPC
    explains it causes headaches in Pokémon.

-   MissingNo --- Not a glitch. Here, MissingNo is a canon entity: a
    corrupted data fragment from the War, a ghost of a Pokémon that was
    never given form. Obtainable via a full quest in Cinnabar.

-   The Surfing Pikachu --- Obtainable legitimately if you bring a
    Pikachu to a specific NPC in Olivine who used to be a surfer. She
    teaches it Surf. It\'s permanent.

-   The \'fourth starter\' --- A Pokémon called Gorochu (Raichu\'s
    scrapped evolution) is hidden in the Safari Zone. It was referenced
    in early Pokémon media as the true final form of Pikachu.

**Pillar 4: Authentic Gen 1/2 Aesthetic**

Everything should feel like it truly belongs in this era:

-   Visual style: GBC color palette, 8x8 tile graphics, authentic sprite
    style for all new Pokémon

-   Audio: GB sound chip (4 channels), original OST with new
    compositions in the style of Junichi Masuda\'s early work

-   Gameplay: Day/night cycle (Crystal), real-time clock events, Gen 2
    move/type mechanics

-   No Gen 3+ mechanics --- no abilities, no natures emphasized, no
    physical/special split (keeping Gen 2 authenticity)

-   Text boxes, menus, and UI are pixel-perfect to Pokémon Crystal

The game takes place across both Kanto and Johto --- not sequentially
(as in HG/SS), but as a unified map where both regions are accessible
from the start with the right HMs and progression unlocks. The world
feels larger than either game alone.

**4. Player & Rivals**

**The Player**

Default names: GOLD / KRIS (can be customized). The player is a quiet
kid who moved to New Bark Town. Their mother says their father \'had to
go away.\' The father is revealed late-game to be a researcher who
worked on the Mewtwo project and disappeared.

**Rival 1: Silver (canonical rival)**

Silver\'s arc is intact --- but expanded. You learn his full backstory
(son of Giovanni) earlier, and his redemption arc is more gradual and
earned. His final team reflects growth.

**Rival 2: ASH (the anime player)**

A cocky kid with a Pikachu shows up in key cities. He references events
from the anime (\'I beat the Orange League!\'). He\'s friendly but
overconfident. His final battle at Mt. Silver is post-game and features
his complete anime team at Lv. 75+.

**7. Story Arc Breakdown**

**Main Story: \'The Lost Things\'**

The main quest follows the standard gym badge structure but is woven
with a larger mystery: something happened 20 years ago that the adults
don\'t talk about. As you collect badges, you uncover it --- the war,
the Mewtwo project, the disappearing researchers, and your father\'s
role in it all.

Act 1 (Johto Badges 1--4): Establishing the world. First hints of the
old war. Meet Silver. First beta Pokémon sightings.

Act 2 (Johto Badges 5--8): The anime events begin. Porygon incident in
Goldenrod. Sabrina\'s backstory in Saffron. Marowak\'s full story in
Lavender.

Act 3 (Kanto Badges): Cinnabar Lab ruins. Mewtwo\'s full creation story.
Your father\'s journal discovered. MissingNo quest.

Post-Game: The Pokégod encounters. ASH\'s final battle. Gorochu. The
true ending --- finding your father.

**8. Technical Foundation**

**Base ROM**

pokecrystal (pret decompilation) --- the full disassembly of Pokémon
Crystal for Game Boy Color. This gives us access to the actual C and
assembly source code, meaning we can change anything.

Repo: https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal

**Build Environment**

-   OS: Ubuntu 22.04 / WSL2 on Windows / macOS

-   Compiler: RGBDS (Rednex Game Boy Development System)

-   Emulator for testing: mGBA (accurate, fast, GBC support)

-   Map editor: Polished Map (best modern Crystal map tool)

-   Sprite editor: Tilemap Studio

**Claude Code Workflow**

Run Claude Code from inside the pokecrystal directory. It can read,
edit, and validate all the assembly and C files directly. Recommended
workflow:

-   Start each session by telling Claude Code which feature you are
    implementing

-   Ask it to find the relevant files first before editing

-   After each change, run \'make\' in the terminal to check for compile
    errors

-   Test in mGBA after each milestone, not just at the end

-   Use git commits after each working feature: \'git commit -m \"Add
    Mew under truck\"\'

**10. Tone & Writing Guidelines**

The game should feel like it was made by people who loved the original
games but weren\'t afraid to acknowledge their darkness. Key principles:

-   NPCs talk like real kids and adults, not like exposition machines

-   The dark content (war, death, experiments) is implied through
    environment and diary entries, not cutscenes

-   The myths are presented deadpan --- no winking at the camera. The
    game believes in itself.

-   Pokédex entries for beta Pokémon should feel like they belong ---
    use the same slightly dark, strange tone as Gen 1/2 entries

-   Dialogue length: short. This is a GBC game. Three lines per text box
    maximum.

-   The rival ASH is never mean. He\'s just a kid who thinks he\'s the
    best. He\'s kind of right.
